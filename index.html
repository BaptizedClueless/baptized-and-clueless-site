The show

Faith is beautiful. It's also confusing. We talk about both.

Baptized & Clueless is a home for new and long-time followers of Christ who are done pretending they have it all figured out. Every episode is an honest conversation about what actually happens after you say yes to God — the wins, the wandering, and the questions nobody warned you about.

We swap the polished testimony for the real one: crawling, walking, and running toward a deeper relationship with God, one clueless moment at a time.
